John 1:14. And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of the only one from the Father, full of grace and truth.

Calvin, on "the Word was made flesh": "When he tells us that the Speech was made flesh, we clearly infer from this the unity of his Person; for it is impossible that he who is now a man could be any other than he who was always the true God, since it is said that that God was made man."

One subject: the one who became man is the one who was always God.
