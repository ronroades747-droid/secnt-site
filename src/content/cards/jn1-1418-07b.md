John 1:6. There came a man, sent from God; his name was John.

With the Baptist the Prologue moves on from the Word in the beginning. Calvin: "The Evangelist now begins to discourse about the manner in which the Son of God was manifested in flesh".

The Prologue is a line. It goes forward, and its last verse comes back to its first.
