John 1:12. To all who received him, to those believing in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.

Many of us learned it as "power": "to them gave he power to become the sons of God." Calvin, reading John's Greek, heard something else: "The word ἐξουσία here appears to me to mean a right, or claim."

Not a strength we supply, and not a chance left to us to take or refuse. A right, given. The grace of adoption is offered, in Calvin's words, "not by possibility only, but actually".

A right that carries its effect.
