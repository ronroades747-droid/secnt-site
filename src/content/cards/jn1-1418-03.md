John 1:14–18. Where does the last movement of John's Prologue begin, and where does it end?

At verse 14 the Word is named again, for the first time since verse 1. "Only" and "Father" appear together there, and together again at verse 18, and nowhere else in the Prologue. And a new voice enters: "we beheld his glory." Herman Ridderbos sets both edges in one sentence:

"In vs. 14 the third and last segment of the three-part cycle begins, and at the end (vs. 18) everything returns to the beginning."
