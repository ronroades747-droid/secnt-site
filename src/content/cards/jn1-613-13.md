John 1:10. He was in the world, and the world came to be through him — and yet the world did not know him.

How could the world miss its own Maker? Not, Calvin says, for lack of evidence: before Christ came in the flesh, his power was already on display everywhere.

Calvin: "What can be more unreasonable than to draw water from a running stream, and never to think of the fountain from which that stream flows?"

The not-knowing was the world's own. And John does not leave it there: the next verses turn to those who received him.
