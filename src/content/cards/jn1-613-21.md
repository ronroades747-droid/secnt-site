John 1:12–13. To all who received him, to those believing in his name, he gave the right to become children of God — who were begotten not of bloods, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a husband, but of God.

Two verses, two agents. The Word gives the right; God begets. And the giving issues from the begetting.

Calvin: "our being reckoned the sons of God does not belong to our nature, and does not proceed from us, but because God begat us willingly, (James 1:18,) that is, from undeserved love."

The God who begets is the God of the Prologue's first verse. And John does not yet call him the believers' Father. That title waits.
