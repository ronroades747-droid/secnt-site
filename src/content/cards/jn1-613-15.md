Many of us learned John 1:12 in the King James: "to them gave he power to become the sons of God." We tend to hear "power" as a strength put into a person, an inner ability to become what we could not be before.

But John's word is ἐξουσία, and it means a right: something permitted, authorized, given.

Augustine saw the generosity behind the gift: "He was born the only Son of God and was unwilling to remain alone." And he kept the line clear: "we were not born of God in the manner in which the Only Begotten was born of him, but we were adopted by his grace."

Given to them, not put into them.
