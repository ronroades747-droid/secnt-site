John 1:1. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

Calvin on the Word with God: "And this is the eternal generation, which, during a period of infinite extent before the foundation of the world, lay hid in God, so to speak—which, for a long succession of years, was obscurely shadowed out to the Fathers under the Law, and at length was more fully manifested in flesh."

The Prologue begins with the Word who was with God, and it closes on the only Son, himself God, who has made him known.
