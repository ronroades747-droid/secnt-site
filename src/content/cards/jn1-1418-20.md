John 1:16. For from his fullness we have all received — grace in place of grace.

Calvin, on "grace for grace": "For my own part, I agree with the opinion of those who say that we are watered with the graces which were poured out on Christ; for what we receive from Christ he does not bestow upon us as being God, but the Father communicated to him what would flow to us as through a channel."

Every grace we receive comes to us from the fullness of the Son, and that fullness is never spent.
