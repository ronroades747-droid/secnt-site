John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Some Bibles read "the only Son" here, and some "the only God". Origen, writing his commentary on John in the third century, read "God":

"No one has ever seen God; the only begotten God who is in the bosom of the Father has declared him."

The two earliest copies of the Gospel agree with him.
