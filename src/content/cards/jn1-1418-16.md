John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Calvin, on this verse: "For who will dare to affirm that the Son entered his Father's bosom for the first time, when he came down from heaven to assume human nature?"

He has made God known from where he has always been: at the Father's side.
