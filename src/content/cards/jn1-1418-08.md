John 1:14. And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory.

Calvin on what the disciples saw: "the majesty of God was not annihilated, though it was surrounded by flesh; it was indeed concealed under the low condition of the flesh, but so as to cause its splendour to be seen."

The glory they beheld in the flesh is the light that was in the Word from the beginning, now made manifest.
