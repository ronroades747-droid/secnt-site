John 1:14. And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of the only one from the Father, full of grace and truth.

Calvin, on "dwelt among us": "He means nothing else than that Christ discharged on the earth the office which had been appointed to him; or, that he did not merely appear for a single moment, but that he conversed among men until he completed the course of his office."

The Word did not pass through. He stayed, and his own beheld his glory through the whole of his life among us.
