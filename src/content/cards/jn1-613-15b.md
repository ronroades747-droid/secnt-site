John 1:12. But as many as received him — to them he gave the right to become children of God; that is, to those who believe in his name.

John's word for what was given is ἐξουσία. It is not an inner power to be worked up, but a right: a standing granted by the one who alone had it to grant. Calvin read it that way, and he said where the right comes from. Christ is the only-begotten Son, and the honour of being God's children belongs to us only as we are joined to him.

Calvin: "Having been ingrafted into Christ by faith, we obtain the right of adoption, so as to be the sons of God."

And the right is not an open possibility left for us to take up later. Those who believe are already, as Calvin says, "in reality the sons of God."
