John 1:4. "In him was life." Why was, and not is?

Ridderbos: "The past tense is consistently used (six times in vss. 1–4), but this does not mean that the reference is to a state of affairs that is past. Rather, the tense is to be understood from the perspective of one who looks at the present in light of its origin, which is antecedent to all human experience."

The life in the Word is not an episode. It is the abiding ground of everything that lives.
