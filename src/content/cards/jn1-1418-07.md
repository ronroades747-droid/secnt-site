John 1:1–18. Is the Prologue a chiasm, folded around a centre, or a line?

Carson leaves the first question open and holds the second: "Whether or not John intended his readers to find a chiasm in his Prologue, he clearly expected them to detect a certain progression in his line of thought."

The Prologue goes forward, from the Word in the beginning to the Son who has made the Father known.
