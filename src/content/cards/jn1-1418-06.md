John 1:18. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Why "the bosom of the Father"? Calvin says the metaphor is borrowed from men, "who are said to receive into their bosom those to whom they communicate all their secrets."

Calvin: "The breast is the seat of counsel. He therefore shows that the Son was acquainted with the most hidden secrets of his Father, in order to inform us that we have the breast of God, as it were, laid open to us in the Gospel."

At the Prologue's end the Son is where the Word was at its beginning: with God. And the one who knows the Father's heart is the one who has made him known.
