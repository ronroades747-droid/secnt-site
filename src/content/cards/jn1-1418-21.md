John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Calvin, on "No man hath seen God": "When he says that no man hath seen God, we must not understand him to refer to the outward perception of the bodily eye; for he means generally, that as God dwells in inaccessible light, (1 Tim. 6:16,) he cannot be known but in Christ, who is his lively image."

The God no one has seen is known in his Son, who has made him known.
