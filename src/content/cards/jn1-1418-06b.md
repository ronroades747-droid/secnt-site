John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

No one has ever seen God, and yet the Prologue ends with God made known. Calvin: "It is therefore a fixed principle, that God, who was formerly invisible, hath now made himself visible in Christ."

The one through whom all things were made is the one who has made the Father known.
