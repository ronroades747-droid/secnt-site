John 1:12–13. To all who received him, to those believing in his name, he gave the right to become children of God — who were begotten not of bloods, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a husband, but of God.

Most of us hear "eternal life" as a promise for later. But the begetting in verse 13 is already done: one completed act, coincident with the reception. And what it begins is a life.

Ridderbos: "a totally new mode of existence … which as 'eternal life' has already been initiated by the work of Christ".

It is the one life the Prologue has traced since its first verses, run on into the believer. Eternal life, already begun.
