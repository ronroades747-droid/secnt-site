John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Calvin on the bosom of the Father: the metaphor is of one who shares all his secrets. "He therefore shows that the Son was acquainted with the most hidden secrets of his Father, in order to inform us that we have the breast of God, as it were, laid open to us in the Gospel."

The Son is where he always is, and he has made the Father known.
