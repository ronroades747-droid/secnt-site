John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

Calvin on why the verse is added here, to confirm what went before: "the knowledge of God is the door by which we enter into the enjoyment of all blessings; and as it is by Christ alone that God makes himself known to us, hence too it follows that we ought to seek all things from Christ."

The Son is where he always is, and he has made the Father known.
