John 1:14. … glory as of the only one from the Father, full of grace and truth.

Calvin on those words: "the fulness of grace in Christ is the fountain from which all of us must draw."

The first witnesses beheld that fullness in the Son. Two verses on, the whole church says what it has received from it: "For from his fullness we have all received — grace in place of grace."
