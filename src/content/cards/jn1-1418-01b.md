John 1:18. No one has ever seen God. The only Son, himself God, who is in the bosom of the Father — he has made him known.

The Son did not first come to the Father's side when he came down to us. "Who is in the bosom of the Father" holds without restriction of time, his life on earth included.

Bavinck: "… even though walking about on earth, he still continues to be ‘in the bosom of the Father’ …"

The one who has made God known speaks from there.
