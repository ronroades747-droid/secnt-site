John 1:14–18. How are the last five verses of John's Prologue put together?

In three parts. Verse 14 confesses: the Word became flesh, and we beheld his glory, full of grace and truth. Verse 15 is John the Baptist's witness to that confession. And verses 16 to 18 give its ground: from his fullness we have all received. B. F. Westcott drew the same three parts:

"St John gives first the substance of the apostolic witness (v. 14); and then the witness of prophecy, represented by the Baptist (v. 15); and thirdly, a general account of the nature of the revelation (vv. 16–18)."
