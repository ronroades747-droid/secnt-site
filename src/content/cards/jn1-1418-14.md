John 1:16. For from his fullness we have all received — grace in place of grace.

Calvin on "of his fullness": "He begins now to preach about the office of Christ, that it contains within itself an abundance of all blessings, so that no part of salvation must be sought anywhere else."

Everything we need we receive from Christ, and his fullness is never spent.
