John 1:14. And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory.

The Prologue has used the verb "became" of the creation and of the man sent from God. Here it is used of the Word himself, and the subject does not change.

Ridderbos: "It did not cease to be the Word that was from the beginning, and ‘became’ does not mean ‘changed into.’ It denotes an identification."

The Word, remaining the Word, came to be what he was not: not changed into flesh, not disguised in it, and not mixed with it. John states the fact, and not its manner.
