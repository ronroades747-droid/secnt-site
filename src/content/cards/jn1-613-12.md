John 1:9. He — the Word — was the true light, the light that illumines every human being, coming into the world.

Why "true"? Not, Calvin says, to set Christ against false lights, but to set him apart from every other light: whatever shines in heaven or on earth borrows its brightness from somewhere else.

Calvin: "Christ is the light, shining from itself and by itself, and enlightening the whole world by its radiance."

The source, not a reflection.
