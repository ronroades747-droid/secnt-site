John 1:10. He was in the world, and the world came to be through him — and yet the world did not know him.

We tend to hear this as a failure to recognize a newcomer. But the one the world did not know was its Maker.

Ridderbos: "The world did not know him, not because he was a stranger but because it was estranged from him, from its origin."

Why a creature made for this light should turn from it, John does not say. The preacher may leave it where the text leaves it, and go on, as John does, to those who received him.
