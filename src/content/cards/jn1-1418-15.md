John 1:17. For the law was given through Moses; the grace and the truth came to be through Jesus Christ.

Calvin on the verse: "And yet it must not be supposed that anything was exhibited by the Law in a manner fitted to deceive; for Christ is the soul which gives life to that which would otherwise have been dead under the law."

The law was God's gift, and it bore witness to the grace and the truth that came to be through Christ.
