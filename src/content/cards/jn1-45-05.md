John 1:4–5. The light of verse 4 is not a light that switches on at verse 4.

Ridderbos: "The light that shines from 'the beginning' … teaches us to understand the end in terms of the beginning, just as the beginning can only be understood in terms of the end."

The Prologue is read whole. The light of verse 4 and the glory of verse 14 are one divine self-disclosure.
