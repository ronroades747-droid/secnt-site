John 1:13. Who were begotten not of bloods, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a husband, but of God.

Three times John says not. Then once: of God.

Cyril of Alexandria, in the fifth century, heard both edges of the verse: "For no longer are they called children of flesh, but rather offspring of God by adoption."

Not of flesh. And of God, but not as the Only Begotten is of God. Cyril saw how carefully John chose his words, so that no one would think believers share the Son's own nature. Begotten of God, by adoption.
