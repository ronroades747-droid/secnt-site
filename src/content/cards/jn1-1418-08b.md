John 1:4. In him was life, and the life was the light of men.

Calvin reads the verse of creation: the life of the Word was given to us as a light. "And since this light, of which the Speech was the source, has been conveyed from him to us, it ought to serve as a mirror, in which we may clearly behold the divine power of the Speech." (His translator's "Speech" is John's Word.)

The light of the beginning is the light beheld as glory when the Word became flesh.
