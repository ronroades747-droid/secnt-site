John 1:15. John bears witness about him, and has cried out, saying, "This was he of whom I said …"

Calvin on those words: "By these words he means that his intention was, from the beginning, to make Christ known, and that this was the design of his public discourses; as, indeed, there was no other way in which he could discharge his office as ambassador than by calling his disciples to Christ."

John's whole work was to point away from himself to Jesus.
