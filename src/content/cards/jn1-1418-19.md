John 1:14. And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of the only one from the Father, full of grace and truth.

Calvin, on "we beheld his glory": "The Evangelist therefore means, that in Christ was beheld a glory which was worthy of the Son of God, and which was a sure proof of his Divinity."

The glory the witnesses saw was not a glory added to him. It was God's own, seen in the flesh.
