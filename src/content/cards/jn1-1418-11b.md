John 1:14. And the Word became flesh …

Calvin on what the church confesses there: "two natures were so united in one Person in Christ, that one and the same Christ is true God and true man."

One Person: the Word who was with God and was God. The flesh he took did not change him, disguise him, or mix him into something else.
