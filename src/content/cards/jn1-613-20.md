John 1:12. To all who received him, to those believing in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.

What kind of right? Not a power put into us, and not a permission left waiting. A standing granted by a competent authority, effectual because of who declares it.

Westcott: not "mere ability, but legitimate, rightful authority, derived from a competent source which includes the idea of power." Calvin: the Evangelist does not mean "any intermediate faculty, or one which does not include the full and complete effect".

The child of God is not made by feeling, or by effort, but by a grant. A right that carries its effect.
