John 1:14. And the Word became flesh …

Calvin on those words: "In short, the Son of God began to be man in such a manner that he still continues to be that eternal Speech who had no beginning of time." ("Speech" is his translator's word for the Word.)

The Word did not stop being the Word when he became flesh. He came to be what he was not, without ceasing to be what he was.
