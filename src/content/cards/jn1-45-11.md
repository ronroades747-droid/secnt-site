John 1:4. "In him was life." Not a life given once and left to run.

Calvin: "… in the creation of the world there was not merely displayed a sudden exercise of his power, which soon passed away, but … it is manifested in the steady and regular order of nature."

Your life is held, not owned. The Word who made all things goes on giving the life they have.
