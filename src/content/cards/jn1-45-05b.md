John 1:1–18. Was the Prologue an older hymn that John took over and edited?

Carson: "If John has used sources in the Prologue we cannot isolate them, for they have been so thoroughly re-worked and woven into a fabric of fresh design that there are no unambiguous seams."

The pattern in these verses is real: it is elevated, rhythmic, and it groups. It is the evangelist's own work, in the text we have.
