John 1:15. John bears witness about him, and has cried out.

Calvin on the present tense: John's witness is "a continued act", and "certainly this doctrine must be continually in force, as if the voice of John were continually resounding in the ears of men."

Into a Gospel told looking back, the evangelist sets one voice in the present. The Baptist is still bearing witness, in the reader's own day.
