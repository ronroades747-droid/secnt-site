John 1:15. John bears witness about him, and has cried out.

Calvin on the cry: the evangelist uses it "to intimate that the doctrine of John was in no degree obscure or ambiguous, and that he did not mutter among a few men, but openly, and with a loud voice, preached Christ."

The Baptist's witness was not a private word. It was cried aloud, and it is still sounding in the reader's own day.
