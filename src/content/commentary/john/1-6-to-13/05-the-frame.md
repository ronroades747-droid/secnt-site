  alt: "Where John 1:6–13 sits within the Prologue: the Foyer (vv. 1–5) on one side, the summit (vv. 14–18) on the other, the movement between them. Two solid brackets arch over the movement from outside it — v. 1 to v. 18, the “bosom” inclusio, and v. 3 to v. 18, the creator who exegetes the Father. The Foyer's inner pair, v. 4 to v. 14 — the life-and-light on one side, the glory beheld on the other — is drawn dashed and dimmed as the pair that encloses the movement, held open until 1:14. Barth's descending ladder is drawn as the same frame seen from the other side — four rungs: the Word with God, the Word and the world, the Word among men, the Word itself flesh — with these verses on the third rung, the Word among men; the descent is a framework, not the whole. The v. 1 / v. 10 form — three clauses joined by καί turning on one repeated noun, ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος against ἐν τῷ κόσμῳ ἦν — is drawn in its form and its pairing dimmed as held."

A frame, not a coupling; the second movement presupposes a third, treated at 1:14–18."
