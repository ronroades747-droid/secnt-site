The first movement's nodes open the arc in a dim register, as inherited — the life in the Word, the light of men, shining within the darkness, unovercome. The reception segment supplies the next nodes: the light's coming into the world (v. 9), its refusal (vv. 10–11), its reception (v. 12), and the begetting (v. 13) as the already-pole of eternal life — a new mode of existence already begun, the begetting its means — with the present participle of v. 12, “to those who believe” — the one verb whose time reaches the reader's present — holding the arc open.
