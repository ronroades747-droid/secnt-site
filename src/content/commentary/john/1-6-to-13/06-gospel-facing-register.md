An inclusio arc joins the reception's last phrase to the book's last sentence: τοῖς πιστεύουσιν εἰς τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ (1:12) ↔ πιστεύοντες ζωὴν ἔχητε ἐν τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ (20:31). At 13:1, where οἱ ἴδιοι returns and the household that refused him is replaced by the household he loves, the turn's realization is drawn dashed and dimmed, held provisional beyond this volume's scope. The movement is drawn as a line with a turn in it, not folded about a centre."
