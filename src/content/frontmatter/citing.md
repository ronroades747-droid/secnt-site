# Citing SECNT

The series is freely available under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0). You are free to quote it, share it, and build on it — in teaching, in publication, in translation, for any purpose — provided you give appropriate credit and indicate any changes you make. This page sets out how to cite the Commentary and the Lectures, and what the license asks in return.

## What the license asks

Attribution under CC BY 4.0 means naming the work and its editor, indicating the license, and linking back where it is practical to do so. Reuse and adaptation are permitted — including commercial use and derivative works — so long as attribution is given and any modifications are indicated. The full terms are at <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

## Citing the Commentary

Every page of the Commentary carries a *Cite this page* block, filled in for that page. It cites the page's current dated version: the text as it stood on that date, kept at its own permanent address and never changed afterwards. Cite the version, and your reader will find the text you read, whatever is refined later.

**Full form:**

> Roades, Ronald E., ed. *Commentary on the Prologue of John*, on [passage]: "[page title]." Systematic Evangelical Commentaries on the New Testament. Version of [date]. [version URL].

**Short form:**

> Roades, ed., *SECNT: Prologue of John*, on [passage] (version of [date]).

## Citing the Articles

Every article page carries the same block. Where an article was first produced elsewhere, as a thesis was, the block names that origin too.

**Full form:**

> Roades, Ronald E. "[Article title]." [Origin, where it has one, e.g. M.A. thesis, Wheaton College, 1974.] Systematic Evangelical Commentaries on the New Testament. Version of [date]. [version URL].

## Citing the Lectures

Each Lecture teaches a section of the Commentary and may be cited on its own. The Lectures are published on the series' lecture site.

**Full form:**

> Roades, Ronald E., ed. "[Lecture title]." Lecture on [passage]. *Commentary on the Prologue of John*, Systematic Evangelical Commentaries on the New Testament. [lecture URL].

## Stable references

The series is built to last, and its addresses are permanent. Each page keeps its address, and each substantive revision is kept as a dated version, listed on the page under *Versions*. Small corrections, such as a typo, are made to the current page without a new version, so a version always shows exactly what it showed on its date. If you cite the current page rather than a version, give the date you read it.
